02 / About

Curiosity, backed by practice.

I’m SENSI0X, a cybersecurity learner interested in how networks and operating systems behave under real conditions. I like building small tools, understanding the underlying protocols, and writing down what I learn so the work can be repeated and improved.

My approach is hands-on: learn the fundamentals, test in a controlled environment, review the results, and document the findings clearly.

Working principle
Test only systems I own or have explicit permission to assess. Learn responsibly, document honestly.
